Ways to work

Choose the delivery model that fits your stage

The right commercial model depends on how clear the scope is, how quickly priorities change and how much ongoing engineering capacity you need.

01

Fixed Project

Best when scope, outcomes and acceptance criteria are sufficiently defined.

Good for: defined websites, portals, focused product releases and modernization phases.

02

Dedicated Team

A focused engineering/design team aligned to your product roadmap.

Good for: continuous product development and teams that need extra capacity.

03

Time & Materials

Flexible delivery when requirements are expected to evolve through discovery and iteration.

Good for: complex systems, integrations and exploratory product work.

04

Technology Partner

Long-term support across roadmap planning, engineering, releases and continuous improvement.

Good for: businesses without a complete internal product engineering function.

Not sure?
