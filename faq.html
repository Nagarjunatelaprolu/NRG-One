FAQ

Answers before the first meeting

A practical overview of how NRG ONE works, what we build and what you can expect.

What does NRG ONE do?

NRG ONE provides digital product strategy, UI/UX, web and mobile development, custom software, SaaS engineering, integrations, cloud, AI/automation and ongoing support.

Can you work with an existing application?

Yes. We can assess the current system, improve specific areas, add features, modernize components or support the product roadmap.

Do you build MVPs?

Yes. We focus on a useful first release with a foundation that can evolve rather than building every possible feature upfront.

Can you redesign an existing product?

Yes. We can work from a visual redesign, a UX problem, or a broader product discovery effort.

Do you provide mobile app development?

Yes. The approach depends on the product, team and platform requirements, including cross-platform or native delivery.

Can you integrate third-party APIs?

Yes. Integration work can include APIs, webhooks, authentication, data mapping, error handling and monitoring.

Do you work with startups?

Yes. We can support product discovery, MVP development and the engineering foundation needed for early growth.

Do you work with established businesses?

Yes. Typical work includes internal platforms, portals, workflow automation, modernization and product engineering capacity.

What technologies do you use?

We choose technologies based on the product. Common areas include TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, Python, relational databases, cloud platforms, Docker and AI APIs.

Can you sign an NDA?

Where appropriate, confidentiality can be discussed before sharing sensitive product or business information.

How do you estimate a project?

We break the problem into outcomes, scope, assumptions, dependencies and delivery phases. When uncertainty is high, a discovery phase can produce a better estimate.

Do you provide post-launch support?

Yes. Support can include fixes, monitoring, security updates, performance work and ongoing enhancements.

Can you take over a project started by another team?

Yes. A technical assessment and transition plan can reduce takeover risk.

How often will we see progress?

Delivery rhythm depends on the engagement, but we favor frequent demonstrations, visible priorities and early risk communication.

Do you provide source code and documentation?

Delivery ownership and handover expectations are agreed at the beginning; maintainability and understandable documentation are part of our approach.

Can you work with our internal developers?

Yes. We can augment an existing team or collaborate with internal product, design and engineering stakeholders.

Do you build AI applications?

Yes, where AI has a clear business use case. We emphasize evaluation, guardrails, privacy considerations and human oversight.

How do you handle security?

Security is considered through access control, secure defaults, dependency hygiene, secrets management and environment practices appropriate to the product.

Can you start with just discovery?

Yes. Discovery can be a standalone engagement that results in a clearer product scope, architecture direction and delivery plan.

How do we start?

Send the problem, desired outcome, current situation and any useful context through the contact page. We can then discuss the right next step.

Still have questions?

Tell us what you are trying to build.

We will help you identify the right starting point.
