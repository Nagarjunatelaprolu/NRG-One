Frequently asked questions.

A few practical answers before you start.

What kind of projects does NRG ONE take?

Web applications, mobile apps, SaaS, internal systems, integrations, automation, modernization and ongoing product engineering.

Can you work with an existing product?

Yes. We can audit, improve, modernize or extend an existing system.

Do you provide UI/UX design?

Yes. Product discovery, user flows, wireframes, visual design and design systems can be part of delivery.

Can you integrate third-party systems?

Yes. APIs and integrations are a core capability, including payments, CRM, ERP and operational platforms.

Do you build AI features?

Yes, when there is a clear workflow or product reason. We focus on practical automation rather than novelty.

Can you start with discovery only?

Yes. A discovery sprint can reduce uncertainty before a larger engineering commitment.

Do you provide post-launch support?

Yes. Maintenance, fixes, monitoring and continuous enhancement can continue after launch.

How do we start?

Send the business problem, current situation and desired outcome through the contact page. We will take it from there.
